## Downer 2.2.0

### New
- **Video or playlist?** A YouTube link to a video playing inside a playlist or Mix, such as `watch?v=…&list=…&start_radio=1`, used to download the whole list (sometimes more than 100 videos). Downer now asks first: **Download Video** (the default, or press Return), or **Download Playlist** / **Download Whole Mix**. Nothing is looked up or downloaded until you choose. (#18)
- **Settings → Downloads → Video in a playlist:** Ask Each Time (the default), Just the Video, or The Whole Playlist. **Remember my choice** on the question sets it for you.
- **Mixes are never downloaded whole without asking.** YouTube makes them on the fly, so one link can hold 100+ videos that change each time. Whatever the setting, a whole Mix is always your explicit choice.
- **Large playlists are confirmed first.** A playlist of 200 or more videos, or one too long to list in time, asks once before it starts. If that happens after you've closed the window, the popover opens with the question.
- **A link to a whole Mix** (`playlist?list=RD…`) asks before it downloads.
- **Several links at once:** links that need a choice wait in the queue with **Video** and **Playlist** buttons, plus **All: Just Videos** and **All: Whole Lists**. Other downloads carry on meanwhile.
- **Picked the wrong one?** Swipe the row to queue the other choice, without pasting the link again.
- Links dropped on the menu bar icon open the popover on the question.

### Improved
- A long playlist whose look-up runs out of time now keeps the titles it found, instead of starting without them.
- `embed/videoseries?list=…` links are recognised as whole playlists.
- `--no-playlist` and `--yes-playlist` are refused in Extra arguments with a pointer to the new setting, so the two can never disagree.

SHA-256 is in `Downer-2.2.0.dmg.sha256`.
