## Downer 2.2.1

### New
- **Playlist folders.** A whole playlist is saved in a folder named after it, so its videos are easy to find among your other downloads. Turn it off in **Settings → Downloads → Playlist folders**. Single videos, and "just the video" from a playlist link, are saved as before. (#20)
- **Show in Finder in the menu bar window.** When a download finishes, the button now appears there too, as it does in the main window. (#20)

### Improved
- **Cancelling cleans up.** The unfinished files a cancelled download leaves behind (`.part`, `.ytdl`, the separate video and audio pieces) are removed. Videos that already finished are kept, and a failed download keeps its pieces so Retry can carry on. (#20)
- **The window remembers where you left it**, and its size, when you reopen Downer. (#20)

SHA-256 is in `Downer-2.2.1.dmg.sha256`.
