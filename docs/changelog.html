Downer 2.2.1

Playlist folders, cleaner cancel 27 November 2026 Release page

  • Playlist folders. A whole playlist is saved in a folder named after it, so its videos are easy to find. Turn it off in Settings, Downloads, Put each playlist in its own folder. Single videos, and Download Video from a playlist link, are saved as before.
  • Cancelling cleans up. The unfinished files a cancelled download leaves behind (.part, .ytdl, the separate video and audio pieces) are removed. Videos that already finished are kept, and a failed download keeps its pieces so Retry can carry on.
  • Show in Finder now also appears in the menu bar window when a download finishes, as it does in the main window.
  • The window remembers where you left it, and its size, when you reopen Downer.

Downer 2.2.0

Video or playlist? 15 November 2026 Release page

  • Video or playlist? A YouTube link to a video playing inside a playlist or Mix (watch?v=…&list=…) asks whether you want just the video or the whole list, instead of downloading the whole list. Set a default in Settings, Downloads.
  • Mixes are never downloaded whole without asking, and playlists of 200 or more videos are confirmed first.
  • A long playlist whose look-up runs out of time keeps the titles it found.

Downer 2.1.0

Sign-in, compatibility and self-updating 14 November 2026 Release page

  • Sign in for videos that need it. Optional and off by default, with a consent screen first. Downer always tries without cookies and only uses your browser's sign-in when a site says it is required.
  • Best Compatibility. One switch for H.264 + AAC MP4s that play everywhere. It fixes files that play sound but show no picture in QuickTime.
  • MOV container, next to MP4, MKV and WebM. WebM falls back to MKV when a site has nothing WebM can hold.
  • Extra yt-dlp arguments from a list of common options. Anything else is refused, with the reason shown.
  • deno, recommended for YouTube, installed and updated from Settings, and update buttons for yt-dlp, ffmpeg and deno. Tools Downer downloads itself are checked against their published checksum.
  • Downer updates itself with Sparkle, and waits for running downloads to finish first.
  • Clearer, shorter error messages that say what to do next. Cancelling a link while it is looked up stops the look-up. HTTP Error 503 is retried automatically.

Downer 2.0.3

Fixes 2 October 2026 Release page

  • The panel at the bottom grows to fit Show in Finder after a download, instead of letting it spill out.
  • The resolution, container and quality pickers are back to the original menus.

Downer 2.0.2

Made for the trackpad 2 October 2026 Release page

  • Swipe up with two fingers on the download tray to open it, down to tuck it away.
  • Swipe a row sideways to remove, cancel, retry or reveal it. A long swipe does the main action.
  • Drag to reorder waiting links, and drop links in from your browser onto the window or the menu bar icon.
  • Glass switcher for the download type, swipe gestures in the menu bar popover, and haptics on a trackpad (can be turned off). Reduce Motion is respected, and every gesture has a click, keyboard or VoiceOver equivalent.

Downer 2.0

Liquid Glass and a queue 30 September 2026 Release page

  • Liquid Glass design on macOS 26, a refined material look on macOS 14 and 15, and solid surfaces when Reduce Transparency is on.
  • A download queue. Add videos and playlists while something is already downloading, with one row per video and retry for the ones that failed.
  • Real progress: a progress bar, percentage, speed and time left.
  • Set up from Settings. Downer finds yt-dlp, ffmpeg and ffprobe, and can install them.
  • A smarter menu bar icon, a global shortcut that opens the popover with your copied link filled in, and a new layered app icon.

Downer is free and open source (MIT). Built on yt-dlp and ffmpeg.
